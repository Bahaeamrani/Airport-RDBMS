Introduction

The Airport Management Information System is a relational database system designed for Ercan Airport. The purpose of the system is to manage airplanes, airport employees, technicians, tests, hangars, and maintenance-related activities efficiently.

The system stores detailed information about airplanes and their models, tracks airplane parking history inside hangars, records airplane testing activities, manages airport workers and technicians, and keeps track of medical examinations for traffic controllers.

The database is designed using normalization principles to minimize redundancy and improve consistency and scalability.
